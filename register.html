Register and submit your Samurdhi benefit application in one seamless step.

Step 1 Submit Details & House Photo Register with your NIC, address, income, and upload a photo of your home.
Step 2 Samurdhi Officer Home Visit An officer visits your home and records an official recommendation.
Step 3 Grama Niladhari & Officer Verification Your GN reviews the application and forwards it for ministerial approval.
Step 4 Minister Final Approval The Minister reviews and grants or rejects the welfare benefit request.
Step 5 Receive Samurdhi Benefits Securely Approved payments are disbursed directly and tracked in your dashboard.
